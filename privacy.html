Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what information this wedding invitation website collects and how it is used. This site was built for a personal, one-time family event and is not a commercial service.

What we collect

When you choose to sign in with Google to submit your RSVP, we receive your name and email address from your Google account, as shown to you at the time of sign-in. If you fill in the RSVP form directly, we also collect the phone number, attendance response, and any wishes you choose to enter.

How we use it

This information is used only to:

  • Record your RSVP for the wedding
  • Identify who submitted each response, to prevent unauthorized or duplicate entries
  • Contact you if needed regarding wedding arrangements

Where it's stored

RSVP responses are stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to the family organizing the event. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with any third party or advertiser.

Google Sign-In

This site uses Google Sign-In solely to verify that an RSVP was submitted by a real person using their own Google account. We only request your basic profile information (name and email) — nothing else from your Google account is accessed.
